ShiftPay: Shifts, Jobs & Pay

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the ShiftPay mobile application handles information when you plan shifts, estimate pay, use advertising features, make a purchase, or export your data.

Effective and last updated: 21 November 2026

Plain-language summary: ShiftPay does not require an account and the developer does not operate a server that receives your schedules or pay information. Your app data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it. Third-party Google services may process limited device and usage data for advertising, consent, purchases, analytics, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

1. Who we are

ShiftPay: Shifts, Jobs & Pay ("ShiftPay", "the app") is published by Option F ("we", "us", or "the developer"). For privacy questions, contact optionfmail@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

ShiftPay stores the information you enter locally on your device so that the app can provide its core features. This may include:

  • job names, colors, work patterns, shift cycles, calendar entries, absences, holidays, and notes;
  • hourly rates, premiums, overtime rules, estimated gross and net pay settings, and other calculation preferences;
  • app preferences, language, notification settings, purchase entitlement status, and consent-related state;
  • locally generated backup and export files.

We do not receive this locally stored content on a developer-operated server. ShiftPay does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization.

3. Data you choose to export or share

ShiftPay lets you create backups, export data, and share schedule information. These actions are initiated by you. When you select another app, storage provider, email service, or person as the destination, that recipient or service processes the shared information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not automatically receive a copy.

4. Advertising and consent

The free version of ShiftPay may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to infer an approximate general location;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, impressions, or video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device or account-related identifiers, including the Android advertising ID or app set ID where available.

This information may be used for advertising, ad delivery and measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Where required, ShiftPay uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent and provide privacy choices before personalized advertising is enabled. You can also change eligible advertising privacy choices from within the app's settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. You can also manage the advertising identifier and ad privacy options in your Android device settings.

5. Purchases

ShiftPay may offer a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play to remove advertising or unlock the advertised entitlement. Google Play processes the payment. We do not receive or store your full payment card or bank details. The app receives limited purchase information from Google Play, such as the product identifier, purchase status, and transaction or purchase token, to provide and restore the entitlement and help prevent fraud.

Google processes purchase information under Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications and device access

If you enable reminders, ShiftPay may request permission to show notifications. Reminder schedules are created and handled locally on your device. Android may allow the app to restore scheduled reminders after a restart or app update. ShiftPay does not use a developer-operated push-notification server.

The app does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, call logs, or SMS messages.

7. Legal bases and purposes

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, local processing needed to provide the app is performed to deliver the service you request. Advertising-related processing is based on consent where consent is required, and otherwise on applicable legitimate interests or another basis available to the relevant third-party provider. Purchase processing is necessary to complete and manage the transaction you request. Security and fraud-prevention processing helps protect the app, users, and service providers.

8. Data retention and deletion

Information stored by ShiftPay on your device remains there until you delete the relevant entries, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Backup or export files remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete them from those locations.

Data processed by Google or another service is retained according to that provider's policies and your settings. Because ShiftPay has no user account or developer cloud database, there is no developer-held app account or cloud schedule history to delete. You may contact us if you have a privacy question or believe we hold information about you.

9. Security

ShiftPay relies on Android's application sandbox for locally stored data. Communications performed by integrated Google services are protected in transit using industry-standard encryption such as TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so you should protect access to your device and take care when choosing where to share backup or export files.

10. International processing

Google and other services you choose when sharing data may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards and transfer mechanisms they use.

11. Children

ShiftPay is designed for people managing work shifts and pay estimates and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information on a developer-operated server. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review the matter.

12. Your choices and rights

You can review, correct, export, or delete locally stored ShiftPay information through the app's controls or Android settings. Depending on your location, you may also have legal rights concerning personal data processed by third parties. Advertising and consent choices can be managed through the app where available and through Android or Google settings.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ShiftPay's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes may also be communicated within the app or its store listing where appropriate.
